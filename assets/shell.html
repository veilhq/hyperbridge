Discovery sources

Choose where the discovery feed may pull images from. Everything is off until you switch it on.

Followed sources

Paste an Are.na channel or user link, or a Tumblr blog such as searchsystem.co. The feed pulls from these on every refresh.

Library

Export everything (images, tags, sets, followed sources) to one zip, or import a zip from another machine. Importing adds to this library and skips entries it already has.

Or move new entries over your local network: share on one machine, fetch on the other. Only entries the other library lacks are sent. Traffic is not encrypted, so use a network you trust.
